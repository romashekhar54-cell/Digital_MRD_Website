The Hospital Record Management Challenge
Most Indian hospitals still store millions of patient records as physical paper files — discharge summaries, lab reports, prescriptions, X-rays, and OT notes. These are stored in MRD rooms, basements, and almirahs. Retrieving a single file can take 30–45 minutes. Files are misplaced, damaged, or lost permanently. During NABH audits, missing records create critical observations. And every year, the storage problem grows.
🚀
The NeoLotex Digital MRD Workflow
Our fully managed onsite service handles the entire process for you. A dedicated NeoLotex team visits your hospital on a scheduled basis — collecting, sorting, scanning, and uploading records to the secure Digital MRD cloud platform. No capital investment, no technology headaches, no operational disruption. We handle backlog records and ongoing new records with equal precision.

Benefits for Your Hospital
Staff can retrieve any patient record in under 5 seconds from any device, from anywhere in your hospital. NABH audit teams find complete, timestamped, organized records. Insurance and medico-legal record requests are fulfilled in minutes. Physical storage costs are eliminated. And your MRD department transforms from a cost center into a strategic asset that supports better patient care.

FAQ

Common Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about deploying Digital MRD in your healthcare facility.

How long does the initial digitization project take? +
Timelines depend on your record volume. A typical 500-bed hospital with 1–2 million records completes full digitization in 8–16 weeks. New ongoing record workflows go live within 2–4 weeks. Our onsite team manages the entire process with minimal disruption to daily operations.
Can Digital MRD integrate with our HIS, EMR, or ABHA system? +
Yes. Digital MRD supports HL7 FHIR integration with major HIS/EMR systems (Meditech, Insta, eHospital, HCL, and custom systems). We are ABDM/ABHA-compatible, supporting Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission's unified patient health record ecosystem. Our integration team handles full API configuration and testing.
Does Digital MRD help with NABH accreditation? +
Yes. Digital MRD is purpose-built for NABH MRD chapter requirements (MRS.1–MRS.9). Immutable audit trails, proper record retention policies, role-based access, and indexed retrieval directly support NABH accreditation. Many clients achieve zero MRD observations during their NABH audit after implementing Digital MRD.
Is patient data stored securely and within India? +
Yes. We offer India-region cloud deployment (AWS/Azure) and on-premise options. All data is AES-256 encrypted at rest and in transit, stays within India's jurisdictional boundaries, and is compliant with IT Act 2000, DPDP Act 2023, and healthcare data norms. You retain full ownership of your data at all times.
How is handwritten medical record OCR handled? +
Our AI OCR engine processes printed text at 99.8% accuracy and structured handwritten fields at 92–96% accuracy. For complex handwriting, our trained MRD specialists validate OCR output before final indexing. Every record passes through a quality check (QC) stage before being marked as indexed.
What is included in the service — scanning only, or the full workflow? +
Digital MRD is a fully managed end-to-end service: onsite collection, sorting, high-resolution scanning at your facility, OCR, indexing, QC, and secure DMS upload. Backlog digitization of older records is available as an additional service. Ongoing new record digitization continues on a scheduled visit basis per your chosen plan.
What happens to our data if we stop using Digital MRD? +
You retain full ownership of your data at all times. Upon contract termination, we provide a complete data export in standard formats (PDF, DICOM, HL7) within 30 days, after which all copies on our systems are securely deleted and a deletion certificate is issued.
